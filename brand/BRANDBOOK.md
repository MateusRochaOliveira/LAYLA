# 🎨 LAYLA — Visual Identity & Guidelines

Visual guidelines for **LAYLA** (*Low-cost Autonomously Yielded Leveling Alliance*).

---

## 🏛️ Brand Concept & Storytelling

* **Hypatia of Alexandria (The Logo):** The central icon represents Hypatia—mathematician, astronomer, and pioneer in refining precision instruments like the astrolabe. Her stylized profile symbolizes our commitment to accuracy and open-source innovation.
* **Portuguese Tilecraft (Supporting Branding):** While not explicitly drawn inside the logo, the geometric symmetry, grid structures, and color palette of traditional Portuguese tiles form the underlying aesthetic for our brand assets, UI, and documentation backgrounds.

## ✒️ Typography

The official typeface is **Cambria**. Its classic serif geometry complements the historical nod to Hypatia while maintaining high contrast and readability.

* **Headings & Titles:** `Cambria Bold`
* **Body Text & Descriptions:** `Cambria Regular`
